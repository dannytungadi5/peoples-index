Three steps, zero vibes-only

The meme is the vote. The proof is the price.

1

Vote

A public X poll runs 7 days: one in, one out, or new weights. Poll link and screenshot get pinned as evidence.

2

Price

NAV comes from Basis Board's on-chain mids. No unpriced token gets in. If one drops out, the print says PARTIAL.

3

Share

The weekly card is generated straight from the NAV file. It refuses to render a PARTIAL print without the label.

Rules of the vote

  1. 1 poll, 1 decision: one in, one out, or a reweight.
  2. Candidates need a pool verified on-chain, because an unpriced token can't get an honest NAV.
  3. Results are final after 7 days. A tie goes to the token with the deeper pool.
  4. Every result and poll link is kept public in the artifacts.
