Rules of the vote

  1. 1 poll, 1 decision: one in, one out, or a reweight.
  2. Candidates need a pool verified on-chain, because an unpriced token can't get an honest NAV.
  3. Results are final after 7 days. A tie goes to the token with the deeper pool.
  4. Every result and poll link is kept public in the artifacts.

Vote limitations

X polls are public but not on-chain and are not Sybil-resistant. Multiple accounts or coordinated brigading can distort a result; this prototype does not verify one person per vote and cannot reliably filter new accounts or follower counts. Publish the poll URL, closing time, result screenshot and any observed manipulation with each rebalance. Do not describe a poll as representative of all holders or of the wider community.

Genesis was selected by liquidity, not a community vote. Week 1 voting is planned, not completed. Basis Board is the shared pricing dependency: an error there can affect this index.

Live companion: two primary public Base RPC endpoints (mainnet.base.org and publicnode) are compared byte-for-byte. A third endpoint (BlastAPI) replaces one primary only after a transport failure; it is not a third vote. Two matching responses are required; a mismatch or only one usable response hides the value.
